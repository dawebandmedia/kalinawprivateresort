Antipolo  

Kalinaw means peace. Book it, and the whole resort is yours: no other guests, no passersby, just you and your favourite people.

  • The entire place

    Pool, garden, pavilion, three bedrooms, living and dining hall, conference room and videoke room, for one group only.

  • A heated pool

  • Your own suppliers

    Bring the caterer, stylist and photographer you trust. Supplier fees are minimal.

A day and a night at Kalinaw

  1. 2:00 PM

    The gate closes behind you

    Check-in is at 2 PM. From here until tomorrow, the pool, the garden and every room are yours alone.

  2. 3:00 PM

    First swim

    The main pool and the kiddie pool are both heated, so cloudy afternoons and -ber month weather don’t cancel anything.

  3. 4:30 PM

    Games on the lawn

    Teams run their activities in the garden and under the nipa pavilion. If it rains, everyone moves into the air-conditioned conference room.

  4. 6:00 PM

    The tables are set

    Your own caterer and stylist set up by the pool, under the gazebo or in the dining hall. Kalinaw welcomes your trusted suppliers.

  5. 7:30 PM

    Dinner under the lights

    The courtyard lights come on and the pool glows blue. There are no other guests and no passersby, only your people.

  6. 9:00 PM

    Videoke, doors closed

    The karaoke room is enclosed, so you can sing your heart out without the whole street hearing it.

  7. 10:00 PM

    Quiet hours, warm water

    Antipolo’s 10 PM noise curfew begins. The heated pool stays lit for a quiet swim under the stars.

  8. 8:00 AM

    Slow breakfast

    Morning light fills the dining hall. Cook in the full kitchen or fire up the grill.

  9. 11:00 AM

    Check-out, unhurried

    Check-out is at 11 AM. You can ask to extend for ₱3,000 an hour, subject to the management’s approval.
