Good to know

      
Answers from Kalinaw’s own posts and house rules. See all twelve house rules.

    

    

      

        How much does it cost to book Kalinaw?
        
Rates depend on your date, the size of your group and whether you stay overnight, so the resort sends them by message. Use the form on this page and you’ll get today’s rates and open dates.

      

      

        What are the check-in and check-out times?
        
Overnight stays check in at 2 PM and check out at 11 AM. You can ask to extend for ₱3,000 per hour, subject to the management’s approval.

      

      

        Is the pool really heated?
        
Yes. Both the main pool and the kiddie pool are heated, so you can swim on rainy days and cool -ber month nights.

      

      

        Can we bring our own caterer and suppliers?
        
Yes. Kalinaw welcomes your favourite caterers, stylists and photographers. Please tell the resort about all your suppliers ahead of time. Supplier fees are minimal.

      

      

        Will there be other guests?
        
No. When you book, you get the entire resort. There are no other guests and no passersby.

      

      

        Can we visit before booking?
        
Yes. Ocular visits are by appointment. Send a message on Viber or Facebook to schedule one.

      

      

        Is there a curfew?
        
The resort follows the 10 PM noise curfew. Day guests who stay past 10 PM, or arrive after it, are charged as overnight guests. The videoke room is enclosed.

      

      

        Are pets allowed?
        
Up to two pets are allowed, with pet diapers and crates.

      

      

        Is smoking allowed?
        
Not indoors. There is a designated area for smoking and vaping, and a ₱5,000 penalty applies if any room smells of smoke.

      

      

        Is there help on site?
        
You can hire the caretakers for assistance until 9 PM. The resort asks guests to tip them generously.

      

      

        Our group is bigger. What are our options?
        
For a bigger venue, Kalinaw’s sister resort is Amandari-Antipolo Private Resort. See Amandari on Facebook.
