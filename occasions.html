Celebrations

Celebrating at Kalinaw feels like celebrating at home: private, cozy and intimate.

Kiddie parties by the pool, an 18th under the pavilion, a 75th with the whole clan. If it rains, the party moves into the air-conditioned living area.

Bring the caterer, stylist and photographer you trust. The resort welcomes your suppliers, with minimal supplier fees.

  • Pool, garden or indoor setups
  • Your choice of caterer and stylist
  • Bedrooms for the celebrant and family

Weddings

Small gathering, big moments. Say “I do” at Kalinaw.

When you book for your wedding, you book the entire place, so the bedrooms become your prep rooms and photo spots.

There is room for your family, entourage, glam team and photo and video crew, with naturally lit interiors and backdrops made for pictures.

  • Spacious, naturally lit interiors
  • Beautiful backdrops for photos and videos
  • Comfortable rooms for your loved ones
