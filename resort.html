The resort

The whole place is yours.

A two-storey, modern Filipino-inspired house wrapped around a heated pool, with a garden and nipa pavilion out back. Here is every space you get when you book.

Same courtyard, two moods

Drag the handle to watch the courtyard go from bright afternoon to a blue-lit night.

Day Night

Heated pool & kiddie pool

Warm water, rain or shine

The main pool sits right beside a shallower kiddie pool, and both are heated. At night the underwater lights turn the whole courtyard blue.

House rule: no eating or drinking in the pool.

Garden & nipa pavilion

For games, receptions and long lunches

Out back, a lawn and a nipa-roofed pavilion host games, long lunches, wedding aisles and dinners under string lights.

Lounge sets line the garden wall, and the pavilion keeps tables dry if it drizzles.

Living & dining hall

Air-conditioned, with a long family table

Glass doors open from the courtyard into an air-conditioned living room and dining hall, finished with rattan, warm wood floors and woven wall art.

It’s where parties move when it rains, and where breakfast happens the morning after.

Three bedrooms

Air-conditioned, with bunk and queen beds

Three air-conditioned bedrooms: bunk rooms for the barkada and rattan rooms with queen beds for the celebrants.

Wedding parties use them as prep rooms and photo spots, since you book the entire house.

Videoke & games room

Enclosed, so the neighbours sleep

The air-conditioned entertainment room has a TV and sound system for videoke. It’s enclosed, so the singing stays inside.

There’s also a billiards table and darts in the games corner.

Conference room

With TV and sound system

A fully air-conditioned room with a TV and sound system, set up classroom-style or around one long table.

Hold your session here when it rains, or in the garden when it’s sunny.

Kitchen & grill

Hot and cold water, BBQ grill

A well-equipped kitchen with a long stone-topped counter, hot and cold water dispensers, and a BBQ grill for the ihaw-ihaw.

Cook yourselves, or let your caterer take over.

House rules

Twelve rules from the resort, to share with everyone on your guest list.

  1. All guests are required to register.
  2. Strictly no smoking or vaping indoors. There is a designated smoking area, and a ₱5,000 penalty applies if any room smells of smoke.
  3. Please respect the 2 PM check-in and 11 AM check-out times. You may extend for ₱3,000 per hour, subject to the management’s approval.
  4. Please take care of the furniture, appliances and equipment. Damaged or stained items will be charged accordingly.
  5. Eating and drinking in the pool is not allowed.
  6. Please don’t enter the karaoke room while wet from swimming.
  7. You may hire the caretakers for assistance until 9 PM. Please tip them generously.
  8. A maximum of two pets is allowed, with pet diapers and crates.
  9. The resort follows the national noise curfew at 10 PM.
  10. Day guests staying beyond or arriving after 10 PM are charged as overnight guests.
  11. Please tell the resort about all your suppliers. Supplier fees are minimal.
  12. 24/7 CCTV cameras are in use.
